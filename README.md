# DME Helper

A simple desktop companion for flight simmers on the [IVAO](https://www.ivao.aero/) network. Airport weather, live traffic map, online ATC and your personal flight logbook, all in one window.

> Not for real-world navigation. Data may be delayed. This is an independent hobby project and is not affiliated with IVAO.

## What it does

- **Live map** – see aircraft and ATC positions on the IVAO network in real time, with ATC coverage zones. Light and dark map styles.
- **Airport briefing** – enter any ICAO code (e.g. UUDD) to get the current METAR and TAF, wind, visibility, temperature, flight category, online controllers and ATIS, plus arrivals and departures.
- **Network stats** – how many pilots, controllers and observers are online right now.
- **Events** – upcoming IVAO events for the next 7 days.
- **Flight logbook** – record your completed flights and see your total flight time.
- **Saved airports** – quick access to your favorite airports.
- **English and Russian** interface.

## How to use

1. Download `DMEHelper.exe` from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. Run it. No installation needed, it's a single file.

Requires Windows 10/11 and an internet connection.

## Privacy

DME Helper only reads public data. It doesn't log in to IVAO and doesn't collect or send any information about you. Your logbook and saved airports stay on your computer.

## Data sources

Weather from [aviationweather.gov](https://aviationweather.gov/), network data from the public IVAO API, map tiles from Esri.
